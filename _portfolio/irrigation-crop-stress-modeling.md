## Project Overview

With accelerating global climate change and dwindling freshwater supplies, optimizing agricultural water management requires a holistic understanding of **soil-plant-water-atmosphere continuum (SPAC) dynamics**. This research project focuses on developing climate-smart irrigation strategies, understanding crop physiological responses to drought and soil salinity, and leveraging process-based crop models to safeguard yield stability in water-stressed environments.

By combining field experimentation, root phenotyping, spatial mapping of soil constraints, and irrigation water quality management (such as blending saline and freshwater supplies), this work establishes actionable guidelines for enhancing crop water productivity across semi-arid agroecosystems.

---

## Key Research Highlights & Contributions

* **Root Architecture & Stress Adaptation:** Evaluated root phenes and root system architecture (RSA) parameters to identify traits that optimize soil water and nutrient acquisition during acute drought and nutrient deficiencies.
* **Saline & Conjunctive Water Management:** Quantified the impact of lateral drip line spacing and cyclic/blended saline water application ($C_1S_1$ vs. $S$) on soil moisture-salt distribution, plant growth, and crop yield parameters.
* **Soil Salinity & Geospatial Mapping:** Applied spectral indices and geostatistical modeling to map root-zone salinity dynamics, enabling precision site-specific soil and water management.
* **Crop Yield & Water Productivity Modeling:** Modeled crop growth parameters, leaf area index (LAI), and evapotranspiration demands under deficit irrigation scenarios to maximize Irrigation Water Use Efficiency (IWUE).

## Methodologies & Technical Tools

* **Field & Sensor Monitoring:** Soil moisture/salinity sensors, TDR probes, Eddy Covariance, Drip Irrigation Systems
* **Modeling & Analytics:** Crop Simulation Models, AquaCrop, SWAT, R, Python, SPSS
* **Geospatial & Remote Sensing:** Landsat, Sentinel-2, Spectral Salinity Indices, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS
