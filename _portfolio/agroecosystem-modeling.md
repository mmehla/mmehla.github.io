## Project Overview

This research project focuses on advancing process-based agroecosystem modeling to better predict carbon cycling, greenhouse gas emissions ($\text{CH}_4$ and $\text{CO}_2$), and hydrological fluxes across diverse agricultural landscapes. 

By enhancing, calibrating, and validating modules within the **SWAT+ model** using high-resolution field measurements and eddy covariance flux tower datasets, this work bridges the gap between field-scale agricultural management and basin-scale environmental sustainability.

---

## Key Highlights & Contributions

* **Model Development & Calibration:** Developed and refined process-based SWAT+ modules to simulate complex soil-water-atmosphere interactions and contaminant/flux transport.
* **Empirical Validation:** Integrated continuous eddy covariance flux tower datasets across multiple crop rotations to evaluate model performance under varying climate scenarios.
* **Climate-Smart Insights:** Demonstrated how modified irrigation techniques and cover crop systems impact the water-food-energy footprint and long-term soil carbon storage.
