I am an **early career researcher** specializing in *soil and water conservation*, *conservation agriculture*, and *agroecosystem modeling*, and the sustainable management of agricultural ecosystems and groundwater resources. 

Currently, I work as an **Assistant Research Scientist at Texas A&M AgriLife Research**, where I am actively pursuing my passion for academic research and environmental sustainability. In this role, I am developing, calibrating, and validating **process-based modules for the SWAT+ model**, employing field measurements and eddy covariance flux datasets to simulate carbon and water fluxes across diverse agroecosystems. 

My research interests focus on understanding the complex dynamics of **soil-water interactions, hydrological processes, carbon cycling, and contaminant transport**. I strive to understand how these elements intersect to create more resilient, climate-smart agroecosystems. My broader expertise spans a diverse range of critical topics, including *basin-scale hydrological modeling, agricultural water management, precision crop management, drone imaging*, and the *reclamation of problematic soils*. To tackle these complex challenges, I predominantly utilize *quantitative methods, machine learning, advanced simulation models (such as Aqua-Crop and SWAT+)*, and *Geographic Information Systems (GIS)*, ultimately aiming to improve *water-food-energy footprint* sustainability.

I received my **Ph.D. in Soil and Water Conservation Engineering from Maharana Pratap University of Agriculture and Technology** in India, where I examined crop water footprints and management strategies using Aqua-Crop and SWAT. Previously, I was a **Postdoctoral Research Associate at Texas Tech University**, collaborating with producers through the Texas Alliance for Water Conservation (TAWC) to promote water conservation via **sensor-based irrigation management** in the Texas High Plains.

Additional information about my work can be found in the other sections of this site, which offer deeper insights into the research questions I investigate. Feel free to get in touch for inquiries, potential collaborations, or to share your thoughts.
