I am a passionate early career researcher looking to pursuing career in academics and research, currently affiliated with Texas A&M University[cite: 1, 4]. My educational background is in soil, water, and agricultural engineering, with degrees from top Indian universities[cite: 2]. 

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on advancing sustainable agriculture and exploring the intersection of hydrology, agronomy, and environmental science. Throughout my career, I have leveraged precision technologies, remote sensing, and comprehensive watershed modeling to tackle complex challenges. My areas of interest include:
* Soil and water conservation and watershed modelling.
* Groundwater management and crop water use sustainability. 
* Precision crop management, including sensor-based irrigation, drone imaging, remote sensing, and GIS.
* Conservation agriculture, reclamation of problematic soils, and subsurface drainage.
